ORDER ON MESSENGER

Four simple steps

How to Order

No checkout, no account, no online payment. You browse on this site and order by messaging the MADA team.

01

Browse the collection

Open the Shop page and find the piece you want. Every product shows its price, sizes, colours and availability.

02

Choose your details

On the product page, select your size, colour and quantity, and read the fit and material information.

03

Order on Messenger

Press the Order on Messenger button. It opens a chat with the MADA team in a new tab.

04

Confirm in chat

We confirm availability, delivery details and payment with you directly, then your order is placed.

What to send us

When you message MADA, include these five details so we can help quickly:

  • Product name
  • Size
  • Colour
  • Quantity
  • Delivery location

Payment

There is no online payment on this website. Payment is arranged directly in your Messenger conversation with the MADA team.

Delivery

Delivery coverage, cost and timing are confirmed with you in Messenger for your area.

Returns and changes

Need to change something about your order? Message us in Messenger and we will go through the options with you.
