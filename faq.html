FAQ

Everything about ordering with MADA. If your question is not here, message us on Messenger.

How do I place an order?

Browse the Shop page, open the product you want, choose your size, colour and quantity, then press Order on Messenger. The button opens a chat with the MADA team, where your order is confirmed by hand. See the How to Order page for the full walkthrough.

Is there online payment or a checkout on this website?

No. This website is a catalogue. There is no checkout, no cart and no payment gateway here — orders and payment are arranged directly in your Messenger conversation with the MADA team.

How do I know which size to choose?

Every product page lists the available sizes plus the fit and material details. If you are unsure, send us your usual size in Messenger and we will help you pick.

Do you deliver to my area?

Delivery coverage, cost and timing are confirmed with you in Messenger for your area.

How do I pay?

There is no online payment on this website. Payment is arranged directly in your Messenger conversation with the MADA team.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Need to change something about your order? Message us in Messenger and we will go through the options with you.

An item says SOLD OUT — what now?

Products marked SOLD OUT are currently unavailable, and their order button is disabled. Check the Shop page for the rest of the collection, or message us on Messenger to ask about it.

Do you have a Facebook page?

MADA is promoted through its Facebook page, and Messenger is the fastest way to reach us. You can find us here: MADA on Facebook.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no customer accounts on this site — you never need to register or log in to browse or order.
